Our Story

How we put technology to work.

At Orange & Rockland, technology is a means, not an end. The meters in the field, the systems in the control room and the tools on every desk exist for one reason: to help our people deliver value to our customers. The best ideas come from the challenges our teams see every day.

  • More Time for Customers

    Simplify repetitive work so teams can spend their time where it counts.

  • Clearer Answers, Faster

    Make information easier to find, so decisions rest on the facts.

  • Better Experiences

    Find smarter ways to support employees, customers and communities.
