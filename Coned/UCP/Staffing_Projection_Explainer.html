2026 Staffing Projection

Hourly coverage vs. approved leave across the 5 centers
Data as of Jul 10, 2026

Across the 5 centers, 665 CSRs are projected to cover about 1.16 million phone hours in 2026, based on the current schedule and after breaks and lunches. Approved leave takes away 85,291 of those hours, about 7%. We call those hours Exceptions. This page shows how many people are on the phones each hour, what pulls them away, and how that changes through the year. One thing to keep in mind: most leave for the second half of the year has not been booked yet, so those months look better staffed than they really will be.

How the projection works

One sentence: every Active schedule line in the 5 centers is walked across the 2026 calendar hour by hour, breaks and lunches are subtracted, and approved leave removes the staffed portion of the shift that starts on the leave date. The details live in the collapsibles, and every number here ties to the Hourly_Staffing_Projection_2026.xlsx workbook to the decimal.

Scope & sources
  • In scope: 682 Active schedule lines / 665 CSRs in CUST OPS Cent Day, Cent Night, North, South, and Immersive Enrichment. Other Cust Ops areas do not take calls; their schedules and leave are fully excluded, as are the 15 On-Leave lines.
  • Leave data: 15,631 approved leave-detail rows joined from cf_LeaveRequestDetail → cf_LeaveRequest → master schedule; 13,257 rows in scope → 12,720.5 leave person-days. The Dataverse exports were truncated on their visible sheets; full data was recovered from each file's embedded cache.
  • Leave-type names come from the code → name list supplied on 07/10/26 (the cf_LeaveType export contained no rows).
Leave & break rules
  • Only Approved detail rows count; Canceled and Denied are ignored. One person's same-date bookings cap at 1.0 day.
  • A leave day removes the shift that starts on that date; overnight spill lands on the next calendar day. A half day omits the later half of the shift.
  • Breaks and lunches are deducted from both scheduled coverage and removed hours: only genuinely staffed phone time is counted on either side.
  • Redeployment-style types (Campaign, Loaned Assignment, Shift Swap) count as Exceptions because the CSR is off inbound calls, even though they're working.
Data-quality notes & limits
  • The schedule is a snapshot (07/09/26) applied to all of 2026: no hires, terminations, shift bids, or holiday closures are modeled.
  • Break-window cleanup: 51 unparseable placeholders ("AM X"), 2 impossible 12-hour "breaks", and 1 window entirely outside its shift were treated as no break; overlapping windows were merged.
  • 237.5 leave person-days fall on dates the person is not scheduled, so they have zero staffing impact.
  • 1 CSR = 1 concurrent staffed hour; no shrinkage, occupancy, or call-volume modeling. This is a coverage view, not an Erlang model.
