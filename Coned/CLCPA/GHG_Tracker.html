Methodology

Emission factors from EPA GHG inventory & 40 CFR 98 subpart W for natural gas distribution mains (scf CH₄/mile/yr): cast iron 27,104 · wrought iron 27,104 · unprotected steel 12,138 · protected steel 1,126 · plastic 1,118. Converted with CH₄ density 0.0192 kg/scf and GWP 25 (AR5, 100-yr). Avoided emissions = (old factor − new factor) × miles × 0.0192 × 25 ÷ 1000. Asset life assumed 50 yr per DPS case 20-G-0131. DAC designation per NYS Climate Justice Working Group final criteria.
