=======================================================================
 SHAPE OF THE CANDIDATE SET
=======================================================================
  SILENT in every stored year    : 157   the engine has no way to produce these at all
  DIVERGENT in every stored year : 8   the engine produces a different number every time
  MIXED                          : 8   reproduced in some years and not others
  tables involved                : ["A2","A3","A4","A6","A7","A8","C2","D2","D3","D4","F7","F9","G10","I1","J1","J2","J3","J4","J6","J7","J8","J9"]

  THE MIXED SET IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST. A cell reproduced in one
  year and not another is precisely the cell whose ownership the old
  guard decided from the stored value, so it belonged to the preparer
  or to the engine depending on which year was open.
