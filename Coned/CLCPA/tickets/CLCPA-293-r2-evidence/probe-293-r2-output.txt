      AND IT REFUSES FOR A REASON THIS CHANGE DOES NOT TOUCH. A1s
      total columns are declared DERIVED COLUMNS, and the guard reads
      `!computed.derivedCol(cIdx) && !reproduced` -- a derived column

      predates both rounds of this ticket. So clause 3 holds
      structurally rather than by arithmetic luck.
