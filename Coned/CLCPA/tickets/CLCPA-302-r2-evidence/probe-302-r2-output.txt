  lived on a DERIVED COLUMN of a BODY row: on B2 the engine recomputes
  "Total Plugs", and because B2 sits outside the persist strip by the

  still changes in the store; no history entry now claims it. Nothing
  is attributed to the wrong person, and nothing records the engine.
  The distinct-entry option stays open and needs its own turn, as does
  the recorded B2 PERSIST_STRIP follow-up that would remove the stored
  copy altogether and make the question moot.
