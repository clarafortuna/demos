# CLCPA-290, 241 and 289: one ruling needed before any of the three can ship

**Raised:** 2026-09-19, on `c0f8f1a9e6`
**Status:** STOPPED before code, per the standing rule. Nothing was written.
**Why together:** they are one mechanism. 289 promises a computation that 241
says does not happen, and 290's prescribed fix rewrites published figures.

---

## 1. CLCPA-290 reproduces, and the ticket's own fix rewrites the report

### The gesture, in a real browser

A3 and A4 on year 2098, seeded with their own 2025 rows and the Total row
emptied so the engine must fill it:

| surface | A3/2098 Total row |
|---|---|
| **editor** | `Total \| — \| 2,034,907 \| — \| —` |
| **section page** | `Total \| \| \| \|` -- **nothing computes at all** |
| A3/2025 (published) | `Total \| \| 2,034,938 \| $3.8M \| 22,511` |

The editor matches the owner's report exactly: **one of three**. A4 is
identical in shape.

**A second defect the ticket does not state:** on the section page the Total
row computes *nothing* on a live-calculated year, not even Total Participants,
which the editor does compute. Two surfaces, two different answers to the same
question.

*(A previous re-verification of mine said "none of three". That was
`rowsForDisplay` without `recomputeTotals` -- the wrong surface. The owner's
reading was right.)*

### Why the two average cells are blank

`detectAvgColumns` deliberately refuses to sum any heading matching `avg`,
`average`, `per participant` or `mean`. A3's columns 3 and 4 are therefore
excluded from summing, and **no rule computes them instead**. The blank is the
engine declining to do the wrong thing, with nothing doing the right thing.

### The rule-type pattern exists, and its own discipline rejects the fix

`DERIVED_COLS` already carries `weightedMean`, added by CLCPA-143 for E1's
"Percentage Affecting DACs", with exactly the formula the ticket describes:

```
sum(weight_col * pct_col) / sum(weight_col)
```

Its comment records the acceptance test it had to pass, and why:

> Verified against ALL THREE YEARS, which is the point. 2025 alone would have
> said "simple mean" [...] Checking one year is how the wrong formula gets
> shipped; this is the third time in this codebase that a single-year reading
> nearly did it.

**A3 and A4 fail that test.** Measured against the published totals:

| A3 | col 3 "Avg. Incentives by Participant" | col 4 |
|---|---|---|
| **stored / published** | **3,761,330** | **22,511** |
| plain column sum | 3,761,329 | 22,297.2 |
| weighted mean | 138.7 | 1.9 |
| simple mean | 170,969.5 | 1,013.5 |

A4 is the same shape (stored 2,026,699 against a plain sum of 2,026,699 --
exact).

**The published Total row is the plain column sum of an average column.** That
is the very operation `detectAvgColumns` exists to refuse, and which CLCPA-212
records as a defect in its own words: *"a column that should never have been
summed."* The data is internally inconsistent with the engine's rule.

### What registering the rule would actually do, measured

A scratch copy with `wmean(3, 2)` and `wmean(4, 2)` registered for A3, rendered
through the real display path on the **published** year:

```
A3/2025 today                 Total: ["Total", null, 2034938, 3761330, 22511]
A3/2025 with wmean registered Total: ["Total", null, 2034938, 621.56, 1.94]
```

Following the ticket as written **changes what the published 2025 report shows**,
from $3.8M to 621.56. Nothing was written to the repo; this was measured on a
copy and discarded.

### The ruling needed

1. **Register `weightedMean` and accept the divergence.** Derived years compute
   the honest figure; every published year's total is overwritten by it, and
   the dashboard stops showing the numbers Con Edison filed.
2. **Declare the columns un-totalled.** No rule; the Total row keeps a dash in
   those cells deliberately, on every year. Honest -- an average of averages is
   not a quantity -- and it leaves the published years exactly as filed. The
   editor and the section page would then agree, and the remaining work is to
   make the section page compute Total Participants, which it currently does
   not.
3. **Register a `columnSum` rule that matches the filed figures.** The report
   stays self-consistent and a fresh year reproduces what ConEd published, at
   the cost of contradicting CLCPA-212 and `detectAvgColumns`.

**Recommendation: (2)**, plus fixing the section page so it computes what the
editor computes. It is the only option that neither rewrites published figures
nor re-introduces the summing CLCPA-212 removed. If the average total is wanted
as a real figure, it needs an incentives-total column that A3 does not have,
and that is a data question for Con Edison, not a derivation.

**Whatever is ruled, the same rail carries CLCPA-312, 319, 303 and 322** -- see
section 4.

## 3. CLCPA-289 cannot honestly ship without 241

289 asks for A9's `% Change` pair to be marked `(calculated)` in the template.

`(calculated)` is a promise with a specific meaning here: the workbook tells
the preparer to leave the cell alone because **the dashboard computes it**, and
the importer *skips* that cell on the strength of the same accessor.

241 measures that the dashboard does **not** compute it. Marking the pair
`(calculated)` today would tell the operator to leave blank a cell nothing
fills, and would make the importer skip a value the operator might otherwise
have supplied. That is strictly worse than the current state.

**So the honest fix for 289 lands the percent-change rule type** -- which is
CLCPA-241's build. Per the standing instruction, this is the STOP: **a ruling
is needed on folding 241 into 289.**

If 241 is folded in, the rule is expressible without a new type:
`% Change = (current - prior) / prior`, both operands being named columns
present in A9's own schema -- unlike A3's average, this one *is* a quotient of
two named columns, so it fits the existing `pct` shape and needs no new
machinery. It would also make A9's `% Change` recompute on edit, which is
241's whole complaint.

## 4. The family this engine work is a rail for

Declared as ordered, for the tickets **not** built this session:

| ticket | what it will need from this rail |
|---|---|
| **CLCPA-312** | the same total-row rule dispatch; it must mount as a registry entry, not a branch in `recomputeTotals` |
| **CLCPA-319** | needs the section page and the editor to answer the total-row question identically -- the divergence measured above must be closed first, or 319 inherits two answers |
| **CLCPA-303** | needs the marker accessor (`ingestComputed`) and the compute rule to be driven by the SAME declaration, so a cell cannot be marked calculated while nothing calculates it -- exactly the 289/241 gap |
| **CLCPA-322** | needs per-row versus total-row scope to be explicit in the descriptor, which `DERIVED_COLS` already carries and must keep |

**One mechanism, no per-table copies.** Whatever is ruled for A3/A4 registers
as a `DERIVED_COLS` entry derived from the schema, and the four above mount on
the same registry. The thing to avoid is a special case inside
`recomputeTotals`: that is the branch every one of these would then have to
duplicate.
