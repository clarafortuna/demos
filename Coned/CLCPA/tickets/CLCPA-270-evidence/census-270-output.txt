======================================================================
WHAT A UNIFIED MODEL WOULD HAVE TO DECIDE
======================================================================
  1. Is a total row's LABEL structure in a FLAT table, as it already is
     in the hierarchical family? Today it is not, and that is the single
     largest source of the "three different ways" in the ticket.
  2. Should DELETABILITY follow the same rule as the label lock? Today a
     row can be value-locked and still deletable (0 rows).
  3. isTotal is VALUE-dependent: it is arithmetic confirmation, not a
     structural fact, so a row's protection changes as its numbers
     change. 2 labels already differ between years.
     A structural model would have to choose between the label (stable,
     and what CLCPA-240 round 3 fell back to) and the arithmetic
     (accurate on well-formed data, wrong on a half-filled draft).
  4. CLCPA-205 item 2 (the all-totals tables) is still pending and would
     be decided by the same ruling.
