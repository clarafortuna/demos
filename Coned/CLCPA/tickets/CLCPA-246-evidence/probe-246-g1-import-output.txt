--- verdict ------------------------------------------------------------
  THE TOTAL COMPUTES: 10998, which is 9,999 + 999.
  The ticket DIES with the CLCPA-319 build, and for exactly the reason
  its own audit note predicted: the gap was the recompute path for a
  FLAT NON-STRICT total on a value-less import, and the columnTotal
  rule identifies its row by the SAME anchored suffix that flags it,
  so it reaches the row the strict-label bootstrap cannot.
