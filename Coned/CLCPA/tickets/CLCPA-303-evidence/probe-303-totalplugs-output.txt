CLCPA-303 MEASUREMENT: what each surface says about B2s "Total Plugs"
app.js: working tree

B2 IS TWO TOTALS CROSSING. "Total Plugs" is a COLUMN, summing the plug
types across a row. "Total" is a ROW, summing the categories down a
column. The cell where they meet is both at once, and it is the cell the
headline KPI reads.
