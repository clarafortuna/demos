Create new white paper case

This creates a new case in the 2026 rate case (26-E-0142). You'll start at the summary tab — once classification and identification are filled in, the case becomes searchable to other reviewers.

Transmit to Casework

This will package this case — the white paper narrative, Part 105 worksheet, DAC map reference, SbD assessment, and stakeholder log — and transmit it to Con Edison Casework.

After transmission, this case becomes read-only in the portal. All further legal review, edits, and the eventual PSC filing happen in Casework. The portal will continue to show downstream status pulled back from Casework.

One check still open: public engagement narrative on the DAC / Part 105 tab is flagged for reviewer attention. You can transmit anyway, or fix and re-transmit.
